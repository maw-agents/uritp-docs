# Smith Theatre

Primary performance space in the Sloan Performing Arts Center. URITP produces Mainstage productions here.

!!! warning "Before you design"
    Smith is a blackbox with real constraints that will bite a design late if you learn them late. The non-dimmable relays and the absent west crossover are the two that most often force a redesign in week three.

## Venue notes {#venue-notes}

Notes exist here because it caught someone out. Pick your department.

=== "General staging"

    There is no backstage hallway crossover on the **west side** of the building.

    Outside the theatre, northwest door to southwest door is the longest path of travel of any pair of doors. Performers making that cross must go through the lobby or the basement. Build the time into your transitions, not into your apologies.

=== "Scenic"

    **Sound baffles** are mounted in an unsymmetrical pattern on the south wall, each extending into the space as a pyramid. They are not a flat surface and they are not symmetrical, so do not draft against them as if they were.

    A **traveller track** is permanently hung under catwalk 6. The curtain may be dropped, but the track stays. Plan sightlines and masking around a track that will be there on opening night.

=== "Lighting"

    All **catwalk fixtures are yoked at 45 degrees** because of rail spacing and obstructions in the catwalks. This is a physical constraint of the position, not a convention you can request an exception to.

    Smith is fitted **exclusively with non-dimmable relays**. Conventional fixtures are feasible at small scale with a few dimming options, but a design built around conventional dimming will not survive contact with this room. Confirm your dimming needs with the lighting supervisor before you finalize a plot.

=== "Audio"

    The two **repertory Fulcrums** under catwalk 3 cannot move north or south in the space. They slide east and west minimally under the catwalk and may be refocused.

    Treat their position as fixed when you design coverage, and treat refocusing as the only adjustment available to you.

=== "Directors"

    A **true, complete blackout is not realistically feasible** because of illuminated exit signs. Total darkness can be achieved onstage; negating all light in the room cannot.

    If a moment in your production depends on absolute darkness, redesign the moment. This is a life-safety requirement and there is no waiver.

## Who to ask {#who-to-ask}

Anything structural, rigged, or hung goes through production management before it goes into a drafting file. Questions about what is *possible* are always cheaper than questions about what went wrong.

| Topic | Ask |
|---|---|
| Rigging, hanging, load-in scheduling | Production Management |
| Power, relays, console time | Lighting Supervisor |
| Speaker position, comms, playback | Audio Supervisor |
| Shop time, materials, budget | Technical Direction |
